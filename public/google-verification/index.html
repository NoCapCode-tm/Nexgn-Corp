Nexgn

Nexgn is a digital signature and document management platform that helps individuals and businesses create, send, sign, and manage documents electronically.

What Nexgn Does

Nexgn provides digital document workflows for electronic signatures and document management.

  • Upload and manage documents
  • Create multi-signer workflows
  • Collect electronic signatures
  • Send secure signing links
  • Generate completed documents
  • Maintain signing and activity records

Google Account Data

Nexgn may use Google authentication to allow users to securely create or access their Nexgn account. The Google account information requested is used only for authentication and the Nexgn features explicitly described to the user.

Nexgn does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use Google user data to train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.
